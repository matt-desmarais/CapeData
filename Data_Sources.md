## Primary Data Providers
- **CCC** — Cape Cod Commission: gis-services.capecodcommission.org
- **MassGIS / MassDEP / EEA** — Commonwealth of Massachusetts: arcgisserver.digital.mass.gov
- **US Census Bureau** — ACS 5-Year estimates (2023) + TIGER geometry
- **FEMA** — National Flood Hazard Layer: hazards.fema.gov
- **MassGIS** — LiDAR Elevation Hillshade (2013–2021): tiles.arcgis.com
- **USGS** — The National Map contours: carto.nationalmap.gov
